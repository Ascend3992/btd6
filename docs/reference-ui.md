# Reference UI

The v60 interface follows the supplied screenshot: illustrated lives/cash, outlined round numbers, a wooden shop with blue monkey tiles, a blue selected-monkey portrait, three owned/next upgrade rows, green tier markers, closed-path cards and a red sell button. The game keeps its existing towers, prices, round limit and combat statistics.

[Upgrade panel](reference-ui-upgrades.png) · [Placement](reference-ui-placement.png) · [Phone layout](reference-ui-mobile.png)

- Click a shop monkey to begin placing it. Click the same shop monkey again or press Escape to cancel. Switching monkeys removes the previous preview.
- Move over the meadow to see the translucent model and its range. Green means the spot is valid; red means placement is blocked. Click a valid spot to place it.
- Click a placed monkey to select it, then click it again to deselect. Empty meadow space and the panel’s close button also deselect.
- The star counter shows total damage dealt. The blue info button opens attack statistics. Left/right arrows cycle targeting.
- Each row shows the owned upgrade and next upgrade. Click a next upgrade once to buy it. Open either green Upgrades button to browse future tiers and hover over an upgrade for its description. Green bars count purchased tiers. Closed and maximum paths cannot be purchased.
- The bottom of the panel shows the sale value and sell button. Existing crosspath and unique Tier 5 rules apply.
- The settings button pauses the simulation. Auto-start, round start and game speed remain available below the shop. Hero abilities appear in their panel and the ability bar.

Shop portraits and selected portraits use the actual game models. v61 includes all 15 supplied Glue Gunner icons; other towers still use equipment badges until their files are supplied. [The artwork checklist](ui-upgrade-icons.md) lists all 90 slots, including the completed Glue Gunner set and the next batch of 15 Dart Monkey icons.

The v62 Normal Difficulty pause menu provides an Auto Start switch, separate music/effects volume sliders, Restart and Continue. See [upgrade overview and audio](upgrade-tree.md).
